Verdarii - A Homebrew Race

A nature-bound Elven people from the Xumaria setting, who found refuge among the forests of Eryn Lasgalen and spread across the world wherever nature runs deep.

"Verdarii (Base)" below is the base race and lists the traits every Verdarii shares; the other five cards are subraces that have each grown to reflect the wild region they call home.
